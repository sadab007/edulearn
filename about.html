About EduLearn

Our mission, approach and commitment to student success

Who We Are

EduLearn is an educational platform created to help students learn modern web technologies in a simple and practical way.

We focus on clear concepts, hands-on practice and project-based learning so students build real skills — not only theory.

Our curriculum is aligned with university syllabus (CSE0309 Advanced Web Technologies) and industry requirements.
